Privacy Policy

MultiPhoto Fit respects your privacy.

All photo processing is performed entirely on your device. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share your photos or personal data.

Face Data and On-Device Photo Processing

MultiPhoto Fit uses Apple's Vision framework exclusively on your device to detect the position of faces for automatic photo cropping. No face data is collected, stored, transmitted, shared, or retained. Face detection results exist only temporarily during image processing and are immediately discarded after processing is complete. MultiPhoto Fit does not perform facial recognition, face identification, or create biometric templates.
