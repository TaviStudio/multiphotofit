MultiPhoto Fit

Advanced Face & Group Detection

Crop, resize and fit multiple photos at once. Automatically detects faces and groups for improved framing and professional-looking results.

Features

  • Batch process up to 50 photos at once
  • Two Smart Framing modes: Center Faces and Keep Faces in Frame
  • Automatic face and group detection
  • Automatic or manual surroundings adjustment
  • Automatic orientation optimized for each photo
  • Standard Crop for products, objects, and landscapes
  • Automatic fallback to Standard Crop when no faces are detected
  • Export as JPEG, PNG, or multi-page PDF
  • High-quality 300 DPI output
  • Batch watermark support with text or logo watermarks
  • Save to Photos or directly to custom albums
  • Ready-to-use presets for printing, social media, and business
  • Custom sizes in centimeters, inches, or pixels

Privacy Policy

MultiPhoto Fit respects your privacy.

Photo processing is performed entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell your photos or face-detection data.

Face Data and On-Device Photo Processing

MultiPhoto Fit uses Apple’s Vision framework exclusively on your device to detect the positions of faces in photos selected or captured by you. This information is used for automatic photo framing, cropping, resizing, and image or PDF export.

Face detection is performed entirely on-device and is used only to improve automatic photo framing.

Face-detection results exist temporarily in device memory while a photo is being processed. They are discarded when processing is complete and are never retained by MultiPhoto Fit.

MultiPhoto Fit does not perform facial recognition, identify individuals, authenticate users, track people, create biometric templates, or use face information for advertising or profiling.

In-app purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. MultiPhoto Fit does not receive or store your payment information.
